01 / Stereo-led perception

From model outputs to a useful scene representation.

Stereo-led multi-sensor perception connects model outputs to robot-usable geometry and semantics. I co-designed its processing framework and developed and optimized several capabilities within it.

MY ROLE

Shared framework design; ground reference, thin obstacles, carpet recognition and runtime optimization.

CONTEXT

Multi-sensor perception in consumer robotics.

Functional overviewMy contributionConcept illustrationEngineering outcome
01 / Project scope

A framework that connects complementary capabilities.

Model outputs provide complementary information about depth and object identity. The framework turns them into spatial representations, combining geometric and semantic reasoning with ground understanding, obstacle representation and carpet recognition.

04 / Engineering considerations

Consistency across capabilities, within a runtime budget.

Keep responsibilities distinct

Ground-reference reasoning supports geometric consistency; carpet recognition describes a different scene property. Clear responsibilities make their outputs easier to integrate.

Balance representation and processing cost

The framework must provide useful spatial information while fitting a practical runtime budget. Integration and optimization are part of delivering each capability.

06 / Engineering outcome

Geometric consistency and obstacle representation

The framework gains support for online calibration and point-cloud correction during bumpy motion, more useful thin-obstacle geometry, and carpet recognition from complementary evidence. Adapted classification and runtime optimization support robot deployment.

Technical boundaries

Scene interpretation remains dependent on the quality and availability of observations. Constructed representations should retain the distinction between observed evidence and inference.

Functional views regroup responsibilities rather than reproduce internal implementation. Animations and illustrative geometry are independently created using synthetic data, not product recordings or measured results.
